# Muse Spark Open may write, judge and select Glimmer training data, and an adapter trained on it ships "Built with Muse Spark Open"

ADR 0025 kept Muse Spark Open's Express out of SFT targets, and left the license question open ("Read the license itself before any of its text reaches training data"). For the distill-and-filter approach (ADR 0033), Spark Open is one of the two candidate teachers. It may also write or vary intents and act as the judge. We allow all three uses in `glimmer-tune/`, through Baseten. It stays out of `explorer/`'s request path, as in ADR 0025. We accept the license's consequences for any adapter trained on its output.

## What the license says

Muse Model License, version Muse Spark Open, release date October 15, 2026 ([LICENSE.md](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B/blob/main/LICENSE.md), read 2026-11-04):

- **Grant (preamble):** "the rights to use, copy, modify (including fine-tune), create derivative works, distill, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell copies of the Software; and to permit persons to whom the Software is furnished to do so, in each case subject to the following conditions:"
- **§2, definition:** "“Derivatives” means any AI model created, trained, modified (including fine-tuned), distilled, or otherwise improved [...] using the Software or any outputs or results thereof."
- **§2, attribution:** "Licensee shall prominently display "Built with Muse Spark Open" in the user interface of each product or service" [...] "that uses the Software or any Derivatives, or in the accompanying documentation, website, or settings menu."
- **§1, notices:** "The above copyright notice and this license and the Usage Policy shall be included in all copies or substantial portions [...] of the Software and Derivatives (defined below)." Also: "Licensee's use of the Software shall comply with the Usage Policy, which is hereby incorporated into this license and attached hereto as Exhibit A."
- **§3, Model as a Service:** a separate agreement with Meta is needed above $20M revenue when offering models via API. "This Section 3 does not apply to (a) internal use, research, or any commercial activity that is not Model as a Service, or (b) the Licensee's use of the Software as a component to power features of the Licensee's own product or service that is not a Model as a Service."
- **§4:** "Meta claims no ownership of outputs generated by the Software."
- **Exhibit A (Usage Policy):** "This Usage Policy ("Policy") applies to your access or use of Muse Spark Open and any Derivatives."

The fetch tool limited each quote to 125 characters, so the `[...]` marks text the reader left out. Read the full clauses in the license before relying on the exact wording.

## What the host's terms say

Baseten Terms and Conditions ([baseten.co/terms-and-conditions](https://www.baseten.co/terms-and-conditions/), read 2026-11-04; the page shows no effective date). They add no restriction on using outputs for training:
- **§1:** "“Model Output” means the output of any query or inference request by a Customer Model or a model that is part of a Third-Party Product." Also: "“Customer Content” means (a) any content, data, or information submitted by Authorized Users or End Users to the Services, including Customer Models, and (b) all Model Outputs."
- **§6.1:** "Customer reserves all rights, title, and interest in and to Customer Content, including all intellectual property rights therein."
- **§1, Third-Party Products:** "Third-Party Products that are machine learning models are subject to the applicable third-party provider's license terms, acceptable use policies, and other terms as communicated through the Services or Documentation." §4.4(b): "Third-Party Products are subject to the applicable third-party provider's terms". So the Muse Model License governs.
- **§4.3(e):** "use the Services or the Documentation to build a competing product or service". This bars competing with Baseten's platform. It doesn't touch training a UI model on outputs.
- **§6.3:** "Baseten will not use Customer Content, including Customer Models, Model Outputs, or inference logs, to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop machine learning or artificial intelligence models."

The student's own license doesn't conflict. Muse Glimmer 30B is Apache 2.0 ([model card](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B)) with a separate Usage Policy, and it says nothing about training on other models' outputs.

## Considered options

- **Glimmer self-distillation only:** no license consequence, since Glimmer is Apache 2.0. It stays a candidate. Phase 1 measures both teachers before the choice. Spark Open's baseline (gallery 0.290, ADR 0025) makes it the weaker candidate.
- **Spark Open for intents and judging, but not targets:** this doesn't avoid the license. §2 counts a model "trained [...] or otherwise improved [...] using [...] any outputs or results thereof". Rows whose intents Spark Open wrote, or that Spark Open's judgement selected, very likely make the adapter a Derivative too. So the line is drawn at any Spark Open output in the data path, not at targets.

## Consequences

- **An adapter is a Derivative if any Spark Open output reached its training data:** targets, intents or judge selection. The provenance field on every row (ADR 0033) records this, and each run's manifest must state it.
- **Attribution:** a product that uses such an adapter must show "Built with Muse Spark Open" prominently. The brief asks for the explorer's UI, which §2 also allows to be docs, website or settings. This is the explorer session's to build, and only when a Derivative adapter actually ships. The launch default (base Glimmer + 3 + grammar) uses no Spark Open output and needs no notice.
- **Notices:** copies of a Derivative adapter, if we ever distribute one, must include the copyright notice, the Muse Model License and its Usage Policy (§1). The Usage Policy also applies to the adapter's use.
- **No separate Meta agreement is needed:** §3 doesn't apply to internal use, research, or powering features of our own product.
- **Rules to update:** `glimmer-tune/AGENTS.md`'s "Models" rule and the "Dataset text is ours, not a model's" rule change to match. Glimmer's "only generates A2UI" rule is widened for the judge role only if Phase 3 picks Glimmer as judge.
- **The judge needs images:** layer 4 judges a screenshot. ADR 0025's review notes say Spark Open couldn't see renders in that setup. Whether either model accepts image input must be checked before calibration.
- **Supersedes ADR 0025.**
